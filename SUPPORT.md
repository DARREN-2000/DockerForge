# Support

Need help with DockerForge? We're here to assist you!

## Community Support

For general questions, discussions, and community support, please check out the following resources:

* **GitHub Discussions**: Visit our [Discussions](https://github.com/dockerforge-org/dockerforge/discussions) page to ask questions, share ideas, and connect with other users.
* **Discord**: Join our [Discord community](https://discord.gg/dockerforge) to chat with maintainers and other users in real-time.

## Bug Reports and Feature Requests

If you encounter a bug or have an idea for a new feature, please submit an issue on our GitHub repository:

* [Report a Bug](https://github.com/dockerforge-org/dockerforge/issues/new?template=bug_report.md)
* [Request a Feature](https://github.com/dockerforge-org/dockerforge/issues/new?template=feature_request.md)

Please provide as much detail as possible, including steps to reproduce the issue or a clear description of the requested feature.

## Enterprise Support

For enterprise users requiring guaranteed response times, dedicated support channels, and prioritized issue resolution, we offer DockerForge Enterprise.

Contact us at enterprise@dockerforge.io to learn more about our enterprise support plans.

## Documentation

Our official documentation is the best place to start learning about DockerForge. It covers installation, configuration, core concepts, and advanced usage.

* [Official Documentation](https://dockerforge.io/docs)
